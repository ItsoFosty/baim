# Mehana Stage 1 V1 Image Prompts

Generation mode: built-in image generation with approved project artwork as visual references.

## Background plate

Use case: `stylized-concept`

Asset type: production source background plate for a 1280x720 point-and-click adventure scene,
`scene.chapter1.mehana`.

Image 1 was the approved apartment interior style and rendering reference. Image 2 was the approved
village-square exterior style, Bulgarian architectural language, door/window, and tablecloth
reference.

Create the empty interior of a shabby post-communist Bulgarian mehana: nostalgic, worn, welcoming,
and slightly absurd. Include peeling patterned wallpaper, smoke-stained plaster, a scuffed plank
floor, dusty shelves densely filled with unlabeled rakia bottles, old framed decoration, warm amber
and smoke-yellow atmosphere, high detail, and strong readable value groups.

Use a fixed 16:9 theatrical 3/4 side view. Put the single main entry/exit door at far left and exactly
two old windows on the back wall. Put an old wooden corner bar from back-center to center-right, with
clear counter space for later separate water-jug and sunflower-oil props and one old mechanical cash
register. Preserve large clear staging areas for separate table overlays at left-center and
right-center, plus an open lower walking lane. Put a closed flat rectangular basement hatch in the
lower-right floor, flush and walkable but distinguishable by seams, an iron ring, and slightly
different worn boards.

Background only: no people, waiter, Tony, Bai Mitko, tables, chairs, tablecloths, water jug,
sunflower-oil bottle, glasses, food, ballot box, UI, captions, logos, readable text, or watermark.
Avoid photorealism, muddy grim realism, fantasy-tavern staging, anime, cyberpunk, and glossy
mobile-game art.

## Tony the Fridge

Use case: `stylized-concept`, followed by a chroma-source correction and the project's deterministic
green-screen cleanup.

Create `npc.tony_fridge` as a separate seated fictional NPC. Tony has a massive rectangular torso,
wardrobe-like shoulders, heavy hands, a thick neck, a rumpled dark sleeveless vest over a faded
checked shirt, worn trousers, and a flushed tired face. His eyelids are heavy from rakia, but he is
watchful, stubborn, and comically authoritative rather than unconscious or miserable. Pose him
three-quarters toward screen-left, seated at the vacant rear-right position behind the second table.
One forearm rests toward the unseen tabletop. Include his full seated silhouette, bent legs, and
shoes; the separate table will occlude his lap naturally.

Tony only: no chair, table, glass, bottle, food, room, text, logo, watermark, or real-person likeness.
Keep him visually distinct from Bai Mitko while matching the approved game's character-rendering
family.

The final chroma source used the same approved Tony identity and pose on a uniform vivid green
background for deterministic alpha extraction.
