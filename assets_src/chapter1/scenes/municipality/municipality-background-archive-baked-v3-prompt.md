Created with the built-in image-generation editor. The approved V2 municipality background was the edit target; the gameplay screenshot and `archive-cabinet-v6.png` were placement and design references.

## Final art brief

Make two localized edits while preserving the room, framing, palette, people, signs, windows, floor, lighting, noticeboard, and open floor space:

1. Permanently paint the worn olive-metal archive cabinet into the back-right corner. Match the architecture, lighting, floor contact, perspective, and hand-painted linework. The cabinet has exactly six drawers: closed, closed, third open, closed, closed, closed. Show a narrow left-side plane and no right-side plane.
2. Replace the lower base of the first service counter from the left with an old built-in wooden cupboard about three quarters of the neighboring counter-base height. Divide it into two side-by-side bays with vertically sliding wooden doors. The left door is parked more than halfway upward; the right door is parked more than halfway downward. Worn file folders and thick law books remain visible through both openings.

The cabinet was subsequently enlarged by roughly one third and moved upward into the deeper corner position without changing its six-drawer structure. Final runtime background: 1280×720.
