- around the old counter top, the mask widens smoothly;
- below that line, it covers the complete removed counter bay and built-in cabinet;
- no patch boundary reaches the upper molding, ceiling, or lamps;
- the original closed decorative rectangle remains visible;
- the right archive cabinet remains unchanged.
