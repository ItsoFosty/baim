# Clerk Penka seated character v1

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The current municipality browser render,
Penka's approved chair, and `penka-desk-v10.png` were used as visual references. The built-in tool
returned a baked checkerboard in its attempted transparent outputs, so the accepted design was
converted to a vivid green source and cleaned through the project's deterministic chroma-key
pipeline.

## Character brief

Penka is an unmistakably adult 41-year-old Bulgarian municipal clerk. She was the local beauty
pageant queen 25 years ago at age 16 and still carries herself as though the title remains
administratively valid. She is curvy, shapely, mature, poised, slightly pretentious, polished, and
calmly formidable. Her beauty is expressed through tailoring, posture, makeup, and confidence—not
nudity or sexual caricature.

She has voluminous early-1990s chestnut hair, tasteful period makeup, faux-pearl earrings, burgundy
nails, and a fitted cobalt-blue office dress with red piping and belt. Her seated three-quarter pose
faces screen-left, with one hand ready to align or stamp paperwork. The colors are brighter than the
background staff while remaining harmonious with the municipality palette.

## Production constraints

- Fresh high-resolution hand-painted 2D cartoon-adventure style.
- Strong, readable, expressive silhouette.
- Clearly adult and approximately 41 years old.
- Fictional character with no real-person likeness.
- Full seated figure without chair, desk, room, text, logo, or political symbol.
- Runtime order: chair behind Penka; approved desk in front of Penka.
